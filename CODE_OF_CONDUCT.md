# Code of Conduct

Kiln adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. All contributors, maintainers and participants in project spaces
(issues, pull requests, chat and events) are expected to follow it.

In short: be respectful and welcoming, give and accept constructive feedback gracefully,
and focus on what is best for the community. Harassment, insults, and personal or
political attacks are not tolerated.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the project maintainers at **admin@kiln.com**. All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the
privacy of the reporter will be respected.

Maintainers apply the Contributor Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines)
(correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban) according to the severity of the
behavior.
